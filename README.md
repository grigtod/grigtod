## Hi there 👋

I’m a freelance developer based in London, building Apple Vision Pro, AR, VR, mixed reality and interactive installation projects for agencies, studios, startups and cultural organisations.
 
I work mainly with Unity, Unreal Engine, C#, Python, spatial computing platforms, real-time 3D technologies, hardware integration technologies, AI and custom software for unusual interfaces.
 
Over the last 10+ years I’ve delivered projects across Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest and other VR/AR headsets, Magic Leap, HoloLens, Android and iOS Smartphones, tablets, multi-touch tables, motion capture systems, eye-tracking devices, motion platforms, depth cameras, video walls and smartwatches.
 
Past work includes projects with Atlantic Productions, Digital Catapult, SailGP, Liquid City, Pixel Artworks, Target3D, Passion Pictures, Vision3, Solarflare, Inition, Nexus Studios, London Philharmonia Orchestra, DAZN, Ultraleap, Goya, AND Festival, The Line and other on projects for Meta, Westfield, Cisco, Worldpay, Tata Communications, Stevanato Group, Jaguar Land Rover, Dua Lipa, ARM, Globus Medical, LEGO, Exxon Mobil, Vertical Aerospace, Ford, Renault, National Oceanography Centre, Honeywell, Plymouth The Box Museum, Mary Rose Museum, MAXXI Museum, EE, Ericsson, Emaar Properties, Goodwood Festival of Speed, BP, Fidelity Investments and other. 

My work has also been connected to award-winning immersive projects, including SXSW-recognised work.
 
I’m especially useful when a project needs a developer who can move between prototype, real-time 3D, hardware, interaction design and production delivery.
 
Good reasons to contact me:
 • Apple Vision Pro or spatial computing prototype
 • Unity or Unreal AR/VR application
 • Interactive installation for an exhibition, event or brand experience
 • AI-powered creative tool or physical interface
 • Technical rescue or optimisation for an immersive project
